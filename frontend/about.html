About NyayLens

Making Indian Supreme Court judgments easier to discover, search and access through a simple and intuitive platform.

What is NyayLens?

NyayLens is a legal judgment search platform designed to improve access to Indian Supreme Court judgments. It provides a centralised interface where users can search through a large collection of judgments using case titles, parties, judges, citations and keywords.

The platform aims to make legal information more accessible by combining efficient search, relevant result ordering, filtering and direct access to original judgment documents.

What can you do with NyayLens?

Keyword Search

Search judgments using case titles, petitioner and respondent names, judges, citations and other indexed fields.

Relevance-Based Ranking

Search results are ranked using SQLite FTS5 and BM25 relevance scoring, with additional weighting for important fields.

Year Filters

Narrow your search by specifying a starting year and an ending year.

Sorting and Pagination

Sort results by relevance, newest first, oldest first or case title, and navigate through result pages.

Original Judgment PDFs

Open the original English judgment PDF directly from the search result without leaving the platform.

Case Information

View available case details, including citations, decision dates, parties, judges and disposal nature.

How does it work?

NyayLens uses a SQLite database to store and organise judgment metadata. SQLite FTS5 provides full-text search capabilities over selected judgment fields.

When a user enters a query, the backend retrieves matching judgments, applies the requested filters and sorting preferences, and returns paginated results through a Flask API.

BM25 scoring helps order matching documents by relevance. Original judgment PDFs are accessed through links to the publicly hosted dataset documents.

Data Source and Attribution

The judgment collection used by NyayLens is based on the Indian Supreme Court Judgments dataset published on Hugging Face by labofsahil.

Important Disclaimer

NyayLens is an academic and legal information retrieval project. It is intended to help users discover and access publicly available judicial documents.

The platform does not provide legal advice, interpret judgments on behalf of users, or replace consultation with a qualified legal professional. Users should refer to the original judgment documents and authoritative sources when relying on legal information.
